Task: write ONE line — the single sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting. Judged pairwise by an AI judge on how completely the line kills the meeting and how relatable it is, office humor register.

Approach: brainstorming skill first (at least 5 candidates, pick the sharpest).
